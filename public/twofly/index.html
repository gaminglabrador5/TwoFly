AIRFIELDS

Buy and sell aircraft for AIRLINE MODE. Your fleet can hold up to 10 aircraft. Tail numbers and maintenance are tracked separately for each airframe. Your credit limit increases as you earn certificates. Withdrawals are added to your pilot file balance, while repayments reduce your debt.

The SORTIE LOG records completed and accepted taskings from both FREE FLIGHT and AIRLINE MODE. Clearing the log only removes its entries. Your postmarks and encyclopedia pages are not affected.
