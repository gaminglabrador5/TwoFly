HOW A FLIGHT WORKS

Pick a departure and an aircraft. Issue 1, 3, or 5 taskings. Accept one. Fly that route in the simulator. When you have landed, press Complete. Abort drops it and pays nothing.

FREE FLIGHT

Any aircraft on file can be used. Completing a sortie adds the pay to the pilot file. Certificates and hangar ownership do not apply.

CAREER MODE

Only hangar aircraft are offered. A completed tasking pays money and XP. Certificates unlock aircraft classes and mission types when that lock is on. Home field, maintenance, and the loan apply here.

CERTIFICATES

The ladder is Student, Private, Instrument, Commercial, then ATP. XP moves you up. You can still fly IFR weather without the Instrument Rating, at a penalty to pay and XP. The checkride on the logbook page can earn that rating early.

HANGAR

Buy, lease, or sell. A daily lease payment counts toward the price. Buy it out for the rest, or return it free. The fleet holds 10. Hours, time to service, and health apply to owned aircraft and to leases. Health starts at 100% and is 25% after 40 hours, then keeps falling. At 0% the aircraft cannot take a tasking. A crash sets health to 0%. Service anytime. The bill is higher the worse the health. A serviced aircraft sells for 30% under market. Damage lowers the sale.

CREDIT

A withdrawal adds to your balance and to the loan. The rate is 8% a year, charged only on the amount you borrowed. Interest already added does not earn more interest. Unpaid interest stops once it reaches 25% of the remaining loan, so the balance cannot grow on itself. Repayment clears the interest first. Turn interest off in Settings and the amount you owe stays where it is.

COLLECTABLES

A stamp or postcard unlocks when you complete a sortie that qualifies. Airports count when you depart or arrive there. Cities and landmarks count when the field is close enough. Open an unlocked entry for its page.

LOGBOOK

Certificates, hours, and the latest milestones are here. The sortie list includes Free Flight and Career. Clearing the log does not clear collectables.

WEATHER

The desk shows the real METAR for the field, or the nearest report within 20 miles. Explain This turns that report into plain language. The sky in the simulator can still disagree.

SIM WATCH

Optional, and MSFS only. You can issue, accept, and complete a tasking from anywhere. Sim Watch does not block the flight.

When the simulator is running it can note a crash, bank, G, speed, a go-around, a bounce, the landing time, the touchdown rate, and where the aircraft was when it landed. That position is compared with the planned field. You still press Complete. There is no rating when the simulator is offline.

A crash is the simulator's own crash, a touchdown of 2200 feet per minute or steeper, or 5 G on a hard touchdown. A descent or a steep turn is not a crash. Each completed sortie writes a text report in the logs folder next to TwoFly. Wear and hours apply only in Career, to an aircraft you own or lease.

SORTIE RATING

The letter is the whole sortie, not just the landing. S is 95 to 100, exceptional. A is 90 to 94, excellent. B is 80 to 89, good. C is 70 to 79, acceptable. D is 60 to 69, poor. F is under 60, or a crash. The number under the letter is the total.

Mission is 25. A landing the sim saw at the planned field is full marks. Landing somewhere else still completes the sortie and still pays, and the mission line is lower. The field counts when you are within about a mile and a half, wider for a long runway.

Landing is 30, and it is the largest part. Butter, smooth, firm, hard, rough, or critical. The same rate is judged more kindly in a jet than in a light aircraft. A bounce costs a little. A go-around does not. A firm landing does not damage the aircraft. A crash sets health to zero and the rating to F.

Handling is 20. It comes off for a bank past 70 degrees, a high G load, or speed well above the type. Ordinary bumps are left alone.

Operations is 15. Flying IFR weather in Career without an Instrument Rating costs part of it. The pay and XP penalty is separate.

Efficiency is 10. It compares the planned time with the time until the landing. Being a little off does not matter. It never checks the path you flew, because TwoFly does not follow you on a map.

SETTINGS

Pilot name, company name, and photo stay in Settings. US or European changes runway length, elevation, altimeter, weight, and visibility. Simulator hides types that do not exist for the one you pick. Money, certificate locks, service, and interest can each be turned off. Export a backup before you clear progress.

TIME

The clock at the top is yours. Departure and arrival times on a tasking are local to the airfield. Departure is a suggestion. In Career Mode, a timed arrival pays less if you are late.

OPERATOR IDENTITY is tied to this application. Money and certificate locks are optional — turn both off and Career plays like Free Flight with a hangar. Currency conversion only changes displayed prices. Your CAREER HOME FIELD is the base used by the HOME button. CLEAR ALL PROGRESS permanently erases your pilot file.
