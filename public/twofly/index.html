FREE FLIGHT

Choose a departure airport and an aircraft, then issue 1, 3, or 5 taskings. Pick one, fly the route in the simulator, and press Complete when you land. If you abort the tasking, it’s dropped and you won’t receive any pay.

Any aircraft in the TwoFly aircraft file can be used. Completing a sortie adds the earnings to your pilot file. Certificates and hangar ownership do not apply in Free Flight.

CAREER MODE

Career Mode only offers aircraft you actually have in your hangar. Completing a tasking earns both money and XP. When certificate locks are enabled, earning new certificates unlocks additional aircraft classes and mission types. Your home field, aircraft maintenance, and loan also come into play here. A finished passenger, cargo, or medevac flight can leave one customer review. A crash, an abort, or a reposition does not.

Your career progresses through Student, Private, Instrument, Commercial, and ATP. Earn XP to move up the ladder.

You can still fly in IFR weather without an Instrument Rating, but you'll take a penalty to your pay and XP. The checkride on the Logbook page lets you earn the Instrument Rating early.

AIRCRAFT & MAINTENANCE

Buy, lease, or sell aircraft from the Hangar. With a lease, your daily payments count toward the aircraft's purchase price. You can either buy it outright by paying the remaining balance or return it without paying anything extra.

The hangar starts at 1 aircraft. Private raises it to 2, Instrument to 4, Commercial to 6, and ATP to 10. An airplane you already own stays even if you are over the cap. You cannot add another until you rank up or sell one. Owned and leased aircraft both track flight hours, service time, and overall health.

Aircraft start at 100% health. After 40 hours, health drops to 25%, then continues to fall as the aircraft is flown. At 0%, the aircraft can no longer accept taskings. A crash immediately reduces its health to 0%.

You can service an aircraft whenever you want. The worse its condition, the more the service will cost. A fully serviced aircraft sells for 30% below its current market value, and damage reduces the sale value further.

LOANS & INTEREST

Taking a withdrawal adds the money to your pilot balance and to your outstanding loan. Interest is charged at 8% per year and only applies to the amount you originally owe. Interest that has already been added does not generate additional interest.

Unpaid interest stops accumulating once it reaches 25% of the remaining loan, preventing the balance from growing indefinitely. When you make a repayment, it clears outstanding interest first.

If you turn interest off in Settings, your existing amount owed stays exactly where it is.

COLLECTABLES

Stamps and postcards unlock when you complete a sortie that meets their requirements.

Airports count when you depart from or arrive at them. Cities and landmarks count when the destination or departure field is close enough to qualify.

Once you've unlocked an entry, open it to view its dedicated page.

LOGBOOK

The Logbook keeps track of your certificates, flight hours, and latest milestones. Your sortie history includes both Free Flight and Career flights.

Clearing your logbook does not remove your unlocked collectables.

WEATHER

The desk displays the real METAR for the selected field, or the nearest available report within 20 miles.

Explain This turns the METAR into plain language so you don't have to decipher aviation weather code every time. Humanity has invented enough abbreviations already.

The weather shown in TwoFly may not perfectly match what you see in the simulator.

SIM WATCH

Sim Watch is optional and only available with Microsoft Flight Simulator. You can issue, accept, and complete a tasking from anywhere while the simulator is running. Sim Watch never prevents you from flying.

When connected, TwoFly can detect things such as crashes, steep banks, G-loads, excessive speed, go-arounds, bounces, landing time, touchdown rate, and the aircraft's position when it lands.

The landing position is compared with the planned destination. You still have to press Complete yourself.

If the simulator is offline, TwoFly cannot provide a flight rating.

MISSION RATING

Your final grade represents the entire sortie, not just the landing.

S: 95–100, exceptional
A: 90–94, excellent
B: 80–89, good
C: 70–79, acceptable
D: 60–69, poor
F: Below 60, or a crash

The number shown beneath the letter is your final score.

Mission: 25 points. The Mission category checks whether you actually reached the planned destination. A landing detected at the assigned field earns full marks.

Landing somewhere else still completes the sortie and still pays, but the Mission score will be lower. TwoFly considers you to have reached the field when you're within roughly 1.5 miles of it, with a wider area allowed for longer runways.

Landing: 30 points. Landing is the largest part of the rating.

Touchdowns are classified as Butter, Smooth, Firm, Hard, Rough, or Critical. The same touchdown rate is judged more gently in a jet than in a light aircraft.

A bounce costs a small amount, while a go-around does not hurt your score. A firm landing won't damage the aircraft.

A crash sets the aircraft's health to 0% and automatically gives the sortie an F.

Handling: 20 points. Handling measures things such as excessive bank, high G-loads, and speeds well beyond what the aircraft normally cruises at.

Going past 70 degrees of bank, pulling excessive G, or flying significantly faster than the aircraft's normal cruise range can reduce this score.

Normal turbulence and minor bumps are ignored.

Operations: 15 points. Operations covers how well you followed the conditions of the tasking.

In Career Mode, flying in IFR weather without an Instrument Rating reduces this score. The resulting pay and XP penalty is separate from the rating itself.

Efficiency: 10 points. Efficiency compares the planned flight time with the time between departure and landing.

Being a little early or late is fine. TwoFly isn't trying to punish you for spending an extra five minutes admiring the scenery.

It does not check the route you actually flew, since TwoFly does not continuously track your position on a map.

SETTINGS & UNITS

Your pilot name, company name, and profile photo are managed from Settings.

The US and European unit systems change runway length, field elevation, altimeter, weight, and visibility units.

When a simulator is selected, TwoFly hides aircraft types that aren't available for that simulator.

Money, certificate locks, aircraft service, and loan interest can each be disabled in Settings.

Before clearing your progress, export a backup. Future-you will be considerably less annoyed.

TIME

The clock at the top of the desk uses your local time.

Departure and arrival times shown on taskings use the local time at the relevant airport.

The departure time is only a suggestion. In Career Mode, however, arriving late can reduce the payout for a timed tasking.
