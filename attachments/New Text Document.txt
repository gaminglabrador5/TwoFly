Absolutely. TwoFly already has a pretty good foundation for achievements because you have XP, certificates, money, aircraft hours, maintenance, collectables, airports flown, landmarks, city postcards, and separate Free Flight/Airline modes. The changelog makes it pretty clear that the achievement system already exists under the Collectables section, so I’d make it much more than a generic “fly 10 times” checklist. 

I’d split achievements into categories so they feel like things a pilot actually accomplishes rather than Xbox achievements written by a committee that has never seen an airplane. 😏

### ✈️ Pilot achievements

* **First Flight** — Complete your first tasking.
* **Getting Off the Ground** — Complete 5 taskings.
* **Regular Flyer** — Complete 25 taskings.
* **Seasoned Pilot** — Complete 100 taskings.
* **Old Hand** — Complete 250 taskings.
* **Clocking Hours** — Log 10 flight hours.
* **Building Time** — Log 50 flight hours.
* **Experienced Pilot** — Log 100 flight hours.
* **Aviation Veteran** — Log 500 flight hours.
* **Home Again** — Complete a flight that returns to your home field.
* **Long Way Home** — Complete a tasking more than 500 NM from your home field.
* **No Place Like Home** — Complete 25 flights from your home field.

### 🪪 Certificate achievements

Since TwoFly already has the four certificate levels, these could naturally mirror progression:

* **Student Pilot** — Earn the Student Pilot certificate.
* **Private Pilot** — Earn the Private Pilot certificate.
* **Commercial Pilot** — Earn the Commercial Pilot certificate.
* **Airline Transport Pilot** — Earn ATP.
* **Climbing the Ladder** — Earn two certificates.
* **Professional Pilot** — Reach Commercial Pilot.
* **Top of the License** — Reach ATP.

You could make the certificate achievements special, perhaps with a larger visual treatment than normal achievements.

### 💰 Money / career

* **Six Figures** — Have $100,000 in the bank.
* **Quarter Million** — Accumulate $250,000.

### 🛩️ Aircraft achievements

This is where I think TwoFly could get particularly fun.

* **Type Rated** — Complete a tasking in 5 different aircraft types.
* **Jack of All Trades** — Complete flights in piston, rotor, turboprop, and jet aircraft.
* **Variety Is the Spice of Life** — Fly 10 different aircraft types.
* **One Airframe** — Complete 25 taskings using the same aircraft.
* **Old Faithful** — Put 100 hours on one airframe.
* **Fresh Out of the Hangar** — Complete your first flight in a newly purchased aircraft.
* **Fleet Builder** — Own one aircraft from every available certificate category.

That last group takes advantage of your 40-hour maintenance mechanic instead of treating maintenance as something players just grumble about.

### 🌦️ Weather achievements

This could make TwoFly's weather system matter beyond being a pretty information panel.

* **Clear Skies** — Complete a flight in VFR.
* **Getting Cloudy** — Complete a flight in MVFR.
* **Instrument Rated** — Complete a flight in IFR.
* **Into the Soup** — Complete a flight in LIFR.
* **Weather Doesn't Care** — Complete 10 flights in MVFR or worse.
* **Crosswind** — Complete a flight with strong reported winds.
* **Weather Watcher** — Complete flights in all four flight categories.

You could even have hidden achievements:

* **Not Ideal** — Depart in good weather and arrive in significantly worse conditions.
* **The Forecast Lied** — Complete a flight where the arrival weather is substantially worse than departure.
* **Pick Your Moment** — Complete a tasking during particularly poor weather.

Your current weather system already tracks VFR/MVFR/IFR/LIFR, wind, visibility, sky, temperature and QNH, so these aren't arbitrary mechanics bolted onto the side.

One design choice I'd strongly recommend: **don't make every achievement visible immediately.** Have perhaps 50-70% visible, while the rest appear as silhouettes/question marks. Your current Collectables page already has collapsible albums and achievements beneath the book, so the interface is naturally suited for this. 

I'd also give each achievement three things: **name, short flavor text, and progress**. For example:

> **LOCAL LEGEND**
> Visit the same airfield 25 times.
> `17 / 25`

That would fit TwoFly's existing pilot-file/career approach much better than generic game achievements like *"Achievement Unlocked: Fly 10 Times."*

And I'd make the achievement system track **things the player was already naturally doing**, rather than forcing weird chores. TwoFly already has the ingredients for a surprisingly deep progression loop: **fly → earn → buy → maintain → explore → collect → rank up → fly better aircraft → repeat.** That's a much stronger foundation than simply throwing 100 badges into the UI and calling it gamification.
