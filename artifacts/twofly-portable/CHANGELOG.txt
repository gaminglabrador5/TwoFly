TwoFly — changes since 11:30 AM MDT, 15 Sep 2026
================================================

Pack: TwoFly.exe + README.txt + LICENSE + CHANGELOG.txt
Windows 10/11 64-bit. Double-click. No installer. No Community folder. No sim connection.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
15–16 Sep 2026
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

WEATHER
- Standing-order panel replaced with a weather panel for the selected airfield.
- METAR from VATSIM; nearest station if the field is silent; Open-Meteo as last resort.
- Flight category (VFR / MVFR / IFR / LIFR), wind, vis, sky, temp, QNH.
- Weather stacked under departure (departure / arrival), not a third slab.
- No network: Weather System Offline.

PORTABLE PROGRAM
- Windows 10/11 64-bit TwoFly.exe. Double-click. No installer.
- Desk stays running until the window closes (fixes 127.0.0.1 connection refused).
- Pilot photos baked into the program. stamps/ next to the exe still loads if present.

PILOT FILE
- Local profile: name, airline name, photo, XP, money, hangar, settings.
- Eight marks (1–8). Custom upload still available.
- Top-right chip: photo, name, optional airline, rank, balance.
- SHOW AIRLINE ON PILOT FILE checkbox. Unchecked hides the airline name.

HANGAR AND MARKET
- Cash buy / sell (70% of list).
- Market: passenger-service and dedicated cargo (1+ pax, or 0 pax with ≥4,000 lb payload).
- No gliders, no single-seat types.
- Hangar shows hours and time-to-service. Fleet cap 10.

MAINTENANCE
- Service due every 40 flight hours on that airframe.
- Cannot issue taskings while due. Repair in Hangar for a shop fee.

MODES
- FREE FLIGHT: full type list. Quote shows money only.
- AIRLINE MODE: hangar aircraft only. Quote shows XP and money.
- HANGAR: fleet, credit, market.
- COLLECTABLES: landmarks, city postcards, airport postcards, airfields flown.
- LOGS: sortie log. SETTINGS: operator, money, rank locks, currency, home field.

DESK
- Accept / complete / abort. Player marks complete. No sim connection.
- Brief legs (~30 min) mixed with longer ones.
- Collectables require a completed tasking plus location rules.
