23 Sep 2026 — v1.9.27 Crash detection
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
- A normal descent, a turn, or a wing-low landing is no longer logged as a crash.
- A crash is the simulator crash event, a touchdown of 2200 fpm or steeper, or 5 G on a hard touchdown.
- Wear and flight hours apply only in Career, and only to an aircraft you own. Free Flight and leases do not wear an airframe.
- Each completed sortie writes a text report in the logs folder next to TwoFly.exe.

22 Sep 2026 — v1.9.23 Sortie rating
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
- The score card now leads with a letter: S, A, B, C, D, or F, and the reason under it.
- Five parts: mission, landing, handling, operations, efficiency. The notes say what was seen.
- Landing rate is scaled by aircraft class. A firm landing does not damage the aircraft. A crash still does.
- A go-around does not cost points. A bounce does.

DESK
