WEATHER
- Standing-order panel replaced with a weather panel for the selected airfield.
- METAR from VATSIM, nearest station if the field is silent, Open-Meteo as last resort.
- Flight category (VFR / MVFR / IFR / LIFR), wind, vis, sky, temp, QNH.
- Weather later moved under the departure field so the desk is one screen, not a third slab.

PORTABLE PROGRAM
- Windows 10/11 64-bit TwoFly.exe. Double-click. No installer. No Community folder. No sim hook.
- Desk process stays running until the window closes (fixes 127.0.0.1 connection refused).
- Distribution is TwoFly.exe + README only. Pilot marks are inside the program.
- stamps/ next to the exe still loads if present (sidecar). Stamps zip can be baked in later.

PILOT FILE
- Local profile: name, airline name, mark, XP, money, hangar, settings.
- Eight painted marks (1.png–8.png) baked into the exe. Custom upload still available.
- Pilot block in the top right enlarged (~2.5×): mark, name, optional airline, rank, balance.
- Checkbox: SHOW AIRLINE ON PILOT FILE. Unchecked hides the airline name on the chip and in briefings.

RANKS
- Four ranks from XP: 1 PRIVATE (piston) · 2 ROTOR · 3 TURBINE · 4 TRANSPORT (jet).
- Rank locks on aircraft class, independently switchable in Settings.

MONEY AND BANK
- Task pay posts to the same local account from Free Flight and Airline (when money is on).
- Aircraft-tied notes removed.
- Bank is Farm-Sim style: withdraw / repay against a rank credit cap.
  Rank 1 $80,000 · Rank 2 $250,000 · Rank 3 $800,000 · Rank 4 $4,000,000.

HANGAR AND MARKET
- Cash buy / sell (70%).
- Market lists passenger-service and dedicated cargo types only (1+ pax, or pax 0 with ≥4,000 lb payload).
- No gliders, no single-seat types.
- Hangar shows hours and time-to-service.

MAINTENANCE
- Service due every 40 flight hours on that airframe (simple, Farm-Sim style).
- Cannot issue taskings on an airframe that is due. Repair in Hangar for a flat shop fee.

MODES
- FREE FLIGHT: full type list (hangar / all / class / favorite). Task quote shows money only, e.g. $5,000.
- AIRLINE: own desk (departure, weather, issue, accept, complete, abort). Hangar aircraft that qualify for airline service only. Task quote shows XP and money, e.g. (133xp $5,000).
- HANGAR: fleet, bank, market.
- COLLECTION: stamps, postcards, achievements.
- SETTINGS: XP on/off, money on/off, rank locks on/off.

DESK BEHAVIOR (unchanged contract)
- Accept / complete / abort. Player marks complete. No sim connection.
- Brief legs (~30 min) mixed in with longer ones.
- Stamps and postcards still require a completed tasking plus location rules.

UI

- Removed: airfield count in the header, “hold two seconds” hint, pilots-folder path hint, rank-unlock crib, bank “note” line.
